1. Information we collect

1.1 Account creation & sign-in information

When you create an account or sign in via Firebase Authentication, we collect, depending on the method you choose:

  • Email & password: your email address and an encrypted password (we never see or store it as plain text — Firebase handles the encryption).
  • Google sign-in: the name, email, and profile photo Google provides based on your permissions.
  • Facebook sign-in: the name and email Facebook provides based on your permissions.
  • Phone number (optional at sign-up): used only to link it to your email so you can later sign in with your phone number instead of typing your email each time.

1.2 Data you enter inside the app

  • Accounts & financial entries: client/supplier names, amounts, dates, notes on each entry, and any photo attachment you choose to add.
  • Personal/business details: your name, address, phone, email, logo, drawn signature, or stamp image — used exclusively to appear in the header of PDF reports you generate yourself.
  • Contacts: when you tap the contact-picker icon, the app opens your device's own native contact picker directly. The app only receives the name and phone number of the single contact you choose — it never reads or stores your whole contact list.

1.3 Device permissions the app may request

PermissionPurpose

CameraCapturing a photo attachment for an entry (invoice, receipt, etc.) if you choose to.

Every permission is requested only when you actually use the feature tied to it, and you can decline it — only that specific feature will be unavailable.

Important: Dyooni contains no advertising code and no third-party marketing analytics or tracking tools (no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, or similar). We never sell your data and never share it for advertising purposes.

2. How we use your data

  • Providing the app's core functionality: storing your accounts and entries and syncing them across your devices via your account.
  • Generating PDF and Excel reports based on your data and your personal/business header details.
  • Enabling phone-number sign-in by linking it to your email (section 1.1).
  • Creating backups — local and encrypted, or optionally on your own Google Drive (section 4).
  • Communicating with you about your account when needed (e.g. responding to a support or data-deletion request).

We never use your data for marketing or advertising, and we never build profiles from it for any external party.

3. Voice input & on-device processing

Dyooni's voice-recording feature uses an offline Arabic speech-recognition model (Vosk) that runs entirely without an internet connection, after a one-time download on first use. This means:

  • Your voice is never sent to any server for transcription — the conversion happens locally on your device only.
  • The voice recordings themselves (audio files) are stored locally in the app's own storage on your device only, and are never automatically uploaded to any server.
  • Only the text extracted from your speech (not the audio) is saved with the entry in your account — exactly as if you had typed it.

4. Google Drive & backups

4.1 Local backup

You can create an encrypted backup (AES encryption) protected by a password you choose yourself, saved to your device. We never keep a copy of this password and cannot recover it if you forget it.

4.2 Optional Google Drive sync

If you choose to connect your Google account for Drive backups, the app requests a very narrow permission called drive.file — this literally means the app cannot see or access any file in your Drive except the files it created itself, inside a folder named "Dyooni Backups". Dyooni cannot read any document, photo, or other file already present in your Google Drive account.

We only use the following service providers, necessary to technically run the app — we never share your data with any other party, and never sell it:

We may disclose your data if required to do so by applicable law or a valid legal order.

6. Security & storage

  • Your cloud data is stored on Google Cloud servers via Firestore, protected by access rules (Security Rules) that scope every user's data to that user's own account only.
  • All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • Local backup files are encrypted with AES-256 before being saved on your device.
  • No storage system is 100% secure — we make reasonable technical and administrative efforts to protect your data, but cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you request deletion of your account (see the Data Deletion page), your data is deleted from our servers within the timeframe described there, except where retaining certain data for a limited period is required to comply with a legal obligation.

8. Your rights & choices

  • Access & edit: you can review and edit all of your data directly inside the app at any time.
  • Export: you can export a full copy of your data via the local backup feature.
  • Delete: you can delete an individual account or entry from inside the app instantly, or request full deletion of your account — full details on the Data Deletion page.
  • Revoke access: you can unlink your Google or Facebook account from Dyooni at any time directly from that account's own settings.

9. Children's privacy

Dyooni is a financial accounts-management app and is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under that age. If we become aware that this has happened, we will delete the relevant data promptly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and will notify you inside the app for any material change.
