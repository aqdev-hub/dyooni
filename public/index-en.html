▸ Your privacy

Your data belongs to you

Dyooni contains no ads and no third-party marketing trackers. Your data is stored in the app's own Firebase project (Google Cloud), protected by strict access rules that scope every user's data to that user alone. Read the full details in the Privacy Policy.

▸ FAQ

Questions you might have

Does voice-recorded entry work without internet?+

Yes — Dyooni uses an Arabic speech-recognition model that runs entirely on your device after a one-time download, so your voice is never uploaded to any server.

Is Dyooni free?+

Yes, Dyooni is currently free to use with all of its core features.

How does Dyooni protect my financial data?+

Your data is protected by strict Firebase access rules scoping it to your account alone, and the app contains no ads or marketing trackers. Local backups are encrypted with AES-256 using a password you choose.

Can I delete my account and data entirely?+

Yes, at any time — full details are on the Data Deletion page.
