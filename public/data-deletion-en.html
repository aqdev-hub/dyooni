Deleting your account & data

Last updated: September 2, 2026

We believe your data belongs to you, and you have the right to delete it entirely at any time. This page explains every way you can delete your data from the Dyooni app — whether a single item, or your entire account permanently.

1. Instant deletion from inside the app

You don't need to contact us to delete specific items — you can do this yourself directly, and it takes effect immediately:

  • Delete a single entry: long-press the entry inside the account statement and choose "Delete".
  • Delete an entire account (client/supplier): from the account details screen, open the menu (⋮) and choose "Delete" — this removes the account and every one of its entries immediately from our servers.
  • Delete an attachment or photo: from the entry-editing screen, tap the delete icon next to the attachment.
Note: these actions are instant and final — they cannot be undone from inside the app.

2. Permanently deleting your entire account & data

To permanently delete your Dyooni account — including your sign-in account itself (Firebase Authentication), all your accounts and entries stored in our database, your personal/business details, and any backup the app created in the "Dyooni Backups" folder inside your Google Drive — send us a request through either contact method below.

What your request should include

  • A clear statement: "I request permanent deletion of my account and all my data from the Dyooni app."
  • The email address or phone number registered on your Dyooni account (to verify you are the account owner).

What happens after your request

DataAction
Sign-in account (Firebase Authentication)Permanently deleted
Accounts & financial entries (Firestore)Permanently deleted
Personal/business details & report header dataPermanently deleted
Phone-to-email linkPermanently deleted
Google Drive backups created by the app (if any)Permanently deleted
Warning: this action is final and cannot be undone. Make a local backup first if you'd like to keep any of your data before sending the request.

3. A note about your Google & Facebook accounts

If you signed in via Google or Facebook, unlinking Dyooni from that account's own settings (Google/Facebook) prevents Dyooni from accessing your account going forward, but it does not automatically delete the data already stored in Dyooni's database — you must send an explicit deletion request as described in section 2 above to actually delete that data.

4. Turnaround time

We commit to completing full deletion requests within a maximum of 30 days from receiving your request, and will send you written confirmation through the same contact method once deletion is complete.

5. More details

For a fuller explanation of how we collect and use your data, see the Privacy Policy.
